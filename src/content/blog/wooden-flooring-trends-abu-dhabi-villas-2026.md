Wooden flooring continues to dominate villa interiors across Abu Dhabi, and 2026 brings exciting new directions in colour, format, and finish. At K2 Curtains and Trading, we install engineered wood, laminate, SPC, and parquet flooring in homes from Al Shamkha to Yas Island. Here are the trends our clients are asking for this year.

## Wide-Plank Engineered Oak

Gone are the days of narrow strips. Wide planks — 180mm to 220mm — create a sense of spaciousness and luxury that suits the open-plan layouts common in modern Abu Dhabi villas. Engineered oak is the material of choice because it handles temperature and humidity fluctuations better than solid wood, making it ideal for the Gulf climate.

**Popular finishes for 2026:**
- Natural whitewashed oak
- Smoked grey
- Honey walnut

## Herringbone and Chevron Parquet

Patterned floors are making a strong comeback. Herringbone and chevron parquet add instant character to entryways, formal living rooms, and master suites. In 2026, we are seeing oversized herringbone formats that feel contemporary rather than old-fashioned — perfect for villas in Al Reef and Khalifa City that blend modern architecture with warm interiors.

## Matte and Low-Sheen Finishes

High-gloss floors are fading in popularity. Instead, homeowners are choosing matte and ultra-matte lacquers that hide dust and scratches while giving floors a natural, tactile quality. Oil finishes are also gaining traction for their ability to deepen wood colour over time.

## SPC Rigid Core for High-Traffic Areas

Stone Polymer Composite (SPC) flooring has matured significantly. The latest 2026 collections mimic real wood so convincingly that even designers struggle to tell the difference. SPC is waterproof, dent-resistant, and ideal for kitchens, utility rooms, and homes with children or pets — a practical choice for busy families in Baniyas and MBZ City.

## Warm, Earthy Tones

Cool greys are giving way to warmer, earthier palettes. Think camel, terracotta, warm beige, and mid-tone brown. These colours complement the sandy landscape of Abu Dhabi and pair beautifully with neutral curtains and Arabic majlis textiles.

## Underfloor Heating Compatibility

More villas in Abu Dhabi are installing underfloor heating, and not all flooring types are compatible. Engineered oak and certain SPC products work beautifully with hydronic and electric systems. We always check your heating setup before recommending a floor to ensure long-term performance.

## Professional Installation Matters

Even the most expensive floor will fail if the subfloor is not prepared correctly. Our installation process includes:

1. Moisture testing and subfloor levelling
2. Acclimatisation of planks to your home's environment
3. Precision cutting around columns, archways, and built-in fixtures
4. Skirting board refinement and expansion gap concealment
5. Final cleaning and protective coating application
